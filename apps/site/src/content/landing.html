Overview

Power is the MCP server that builds, checks and hosts full-stack apps — with real code execution and automated gates verifying the work before production.

Your model does the thinking. Power brings the workbench, the database, the secrets, the deterministic gates and the hosting, so every step is checked by something that cannot be talked out of it.

Two decisions stay yours: you approve the plan, and you confirm the publish. Everything else comes back as a receipt.

Open workspace

Power is a remote MCP server. You connect one endpoint to the AI you already use, and it gains the tools to build, check and ship a full-stack app. Power runs the workbench, database, secrets and hosting; the model does the thinking.

Add one remote MCP endpoint to Claude, Cursor, Claude Code or any client that speaks MCP. There is nothing to install and no API key to paste.

You need to be comfortable directing an AI in plain language. Power handles the workbench and the gates; your model handles the code. Two decisions — the plan and the publish — stay yours.

Yes. Free includes 100 build actions a day, three projects and every gate. Refused calls are refunded before the response is sent, so a gate never costs you an action.

Pro is $25 a month with 1,000 build actions a day, unlimited projects and priority on long jobs. Power is $100 a month with 5,000 actions a day.

Plans are monthly. Change or cancel whenever you like. Export stays free on every plan, including your full git history.

Skills carry procedures, questions, checks and validation, and the Project Contract carries your decisions and rules, so agents follow the same method every time.

Yes. Power reduces guesswork by supplying context and validation steps, and pipelines are designed to show evidence and pause for your approval before consequential changes.

Decisions you record in the Project Contract persist for the project and are loaded by every agent, so you do not need to re-explain your architecture in each conversation.

Pipelines are designed to chain steps such as loading context, validation, security checks, browser QA, staging deployment and production promotion. Power runs these steps against your project, in order, and pauses at every approval.

Yes. Production and destructive operations pause for explicit approval, and the approval identifies the action, target and risk under review.

Yes. Pipelines combine skills, integrations and human checkpoints in order, with conditions, retries, loops and fallbacks built in.

Power connects the services your project runs on — development, databases, payments, design, analytics and operations — including GitHub, Supabase, Vercel, Stripe, Framer and Sentry.

Custom MCPs can be added to a project, with the scopes they request shown for review before activation.

Power flags the disconnected integration and pauses any pipeline step that depends on it until access is restored.

Credentials are encrypted server-side with AES-256-GCM and never enter the agent’s context. The browser form shows a name and fingerprint, never a value.

Access is scoped per workspace and project. Permissions are reviewed before an integration is activated, and credential values are scrubbed from tool results.

Plugging in many MCP servers loads every tool definition into your agent’s context. Power is one connection: your agent searches for the capability it needs and loads only that, and it also gets your Project Contract, executable skills and approval checkpoints, not just tools.
